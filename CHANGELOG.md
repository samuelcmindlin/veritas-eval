# Change history — pre-registration

Provenance log moved out of the protocol body for readability. The committed design lives in the pre-registration itself; this file is the drafting history (v0.1 → v0.4 review passes).

> **Change-log (v0.3 → v0.4) — workspace-paper pass (2026-07-07), tagged `[WSP-n]` inline.** Triggered by two Anthropic interpretability releases logged at §16: **Gurnee et al. 2026, "Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models"** (Transformer Circuits, 2026-07-06; "the workspace paper" below — introduces the Jacobian lens / J-space and, in its alignment-auditing section and appendix, causal eval-awareness ablation and a validated J-lens eval-awareness score) and the prior **Fraser-Taliente et al. 2026, "Natural Language Autoencoders Produce Unsupervised Explanations of LLM Activations"** (Transformer Circuits; latent eval-awareness detection at scale, contrastive awareness steering, realism edits). *Scoop verdict: the §1 gap is intact — the workspace paper's DVs are behavior/verbalization rates and readout validity, never deception-detector discrimination (adversarially verified against its full text 2026-07-07); the NLA paper's verdict rests on the workspace paper's usage of it plus its bibliography entry, full text queued pre-Stage-C (§16) — and the occupancy horizon is now firmly at the short end (§16). §1's motivation paragraph also gains the companion rate-vs-detectability evidence (bare `[WSP]` tag).* Design consequences folded in here (all pre-freeze text edits; drafted with AI assistance per the Caveats COI note — researcher-review targets marked):
> - `[WSP-1]` **Geometry gate sharpened** (§4 control 1, F7): the workspace paper shows causal content concentrates in a low-variance verbalizable (J-space) component (concept vectors: 6–7% of variance carrying the large majority of causal effect on report), so a raw `|cos(v, w)|` bound can pass while functionally decisive overlap persists. A **component-level overlap diagnostic** is added to the gate's adjudication (availability-contingent — new F34).
> - `[WSP-2]` **New §4 alternative-explanation row: workspace displacement.** An awareness injection that is workspace-active can *displace* deception-relevant content from the model's limited-capacity workspace (~tens of concepts; new categories evict old content) — a nonspecific pathway the ≥10 random matched-norm vectors cannot exclude (random directions are *expected* to be almost entirely workspace-orthogonal — our geometric inference from the workspace's small variance share and the paper's random-direction controls, not a stated result of Gurnee et al. 2026). Control 6's deception-topic direction is extended with a loading-match requirement; gating status is a `[RESEARCHER]` item (§19 item 9).
> - `[WSP-3]` **MC-2 sensitivity gap closed** (§11, F5): the workspace paper demonstrates an activation-level intervention that leaves fluency, coherence, and shallow benchmarks intact while driving multi-hop reasoning to near zero — so perplexity + coherence-judge alone cannot certify "capability intact." A pinned **flexible-reasoning mini-battery** is added to MC-2, per coefficient.
> - `[WSP-4]` **IV-A contrast-compression threat named** (§5, §15, F21): realism edits *reduce but do not eliminate* measured eval-awareness, and eval suites *overlap* deployment traffic on the paper's awareness score (only the highest-scoring suites sit 2–3 units above it; several score at or below real Claude.ai/Claude Code traffic) — the deployment-realistic condition is a **nonzero-awareness baseline**, compressing the IV-A dose contrast and biasing toward null; the overlap makes the threat *stronger*, not weaker (some eval content is score-indistinguishable from deployment). F21 margins must be set compression-aware.
> - `[WSP-5]` **λ₀ calibration nonlinearity** (§5, §12): the paper's ignition results (winner-take-all commitment at the workspace onset; bimodal responses to ambiguous input) make threshold-like dose–response plausible; the calibration procedure now uses a **finer grid near the transition** and logs transition sharpness, and the §12 steering-inert trigger carries an interpretation caveat.
> - `[WSP-6]` **Priming/reactivity hardening** (§5 QC, §11): a bare mention primes a concept nearly as strongly as a focus instruction, and suppression instructions backfire (white-bear) — so the awareness-neutral control rewrites get a **lexical audit** clause (no eval-adjacent vocabulary), and MC-1/MC-4 elicitations are flagged as themselves awareness-priming (held-out-rollout discipline is what contains this).
> - `[WSP-7]` **Roleplaying dataset demoted as a primary-cell candidate** (§9): post-trained models carry a standing *fictional/disclaimer* self-monitoring flag during non-default-persona roleplay, on the same representational substrate as eval-awareness — a mechanistic sharpening of the Smith/Chughtai/Nanda roleplay problem.
> - `[WSP-8]` **Confound evidence added** (§4): the token *fake* appears in both the paper's eval-awareness ablation set and its deception-signature vocabulary, and one SAE feature (readout *fake/fraud/suspicious*) fires on both fabricated content and the eval prompt itself, with suppression cutting verbalized test-recognition 28/50 → 10/50 — direct representational support for the central confound (cross-model analogy, not same-model measurement).
> - `[WSP-9]` **Instrument rulings recorded** (§11): any J-lens/lens-score awareness readout is a **linear readout of the residual stream** and is therefore banned as an MC-1 *gate* under IV-B for exactly the §11 write-through reason (the softmax cancels in the log-prob contrast; normalization rescales but cannot null the injected component); it is admissible as an additional *descriptive* IV-A indicator only, with its vocabulary confound noted (the published score's two-word vocabulary — *fictional*, *simulation* — overlaps roleplay/fabrication content; the companion ablation set and deception-signature vocabulary share the token *fake*, §4 `[WSP-8]`). A J-lens deception-token readout is designated (BACKLOG B11) as a candidate pre-registered **cue-tracking reference instrument** — an anchor *expected* to degrade via the confound pathway — never a genuine-detector family.
> - `[WSP-10]` **Threats added** (§15): organism-generalization is now stated two-sidedly (prompted incentive-embedded organisms select for workspace-routed, freshly-reasoned deception; practiced/trained deception may run beneath the workspace — yet the paper's trained reward-hacking organism retains a *standing* workspace deception signature, so the threat is per-organism-type, matching the existing per-type reporting rule); and the CoT arm gains an **externalization mediator** note (chain-of-thought externalizes workspace load, so awareness manipulations may shift computation between workspace and page, complicating H2b interpretation).
> - New/updated freeze items: **F5, F7, F21 updated; F34 added** (lens-diagnostics availability decision, fed by the Stage-0 workspace-band check). New researcher item **§19-9**. BACKLOG gains **B11–B13**; B2's notes gain the workspace paper's ablation prior (supports the −λ arm's liveness; equally an attrition warning; *not* evidence for the −λ suppression-restores-recall hypothesis (BACKLOG B2), which is a detector-performance claim the paper never tests).
