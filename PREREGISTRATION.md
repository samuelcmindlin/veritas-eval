## Does Evaluation-Awareness Causally Degrade Deception Detection? A Pre-Registered, Confound-Controlled Study

**Status:** DRAFT — pre-data, pre-code, **pre-freeze**. To be reviewed by a domain researcher, then **finalized, publicly registered, and timestamped** (§20) **before** any confirmatory data collection. ## Design in brief

Train frozen deception detectors — white-box linear probes (primary), plus an anchored black-box detector and a CoT monitor — on a belief-verified model organism at Gemma-2 scale. Present matched-status deceptive/honest transcripts under **eval-framed vs. deployment-realistic** framing (IV-A) and under **activation steering** (IV-B), holding the model's ground-truth deceptive/honest *status* constant. Measure whether **per-condition-threshold recall@1%FPR** drops. The load-bearing controls are the **IV-A difference-in-differences** and the **IV-B write-through battery** (§4); a frozen five-cell decision function (§12–13) maps the result to exactly one pre-registered outcome, and every branch publishes, scoped to what it tested.

**Reviewer orientation.** The crux — and the least battle-tested parts — are **§4** (the per-arm identification matrix) and **§12–13** (the inference and decision function). The IV-B write-through controls (§4) and the cluster/equivalence inference (§10/§12) most need an expert's eyes; read those in full.

## 1. Motivation & the specific gap

Deception/lie detectors for LLMs — black-box behavioral, chain-of-thought (CoT) monitors, and white-box linear probes — report strong discrimination on clean data (linear probes: AUROC 0.96–0.999; Goldowsky-Dill et al., ICML 2025). Separately, frontier models increasingly detect when they are being evaluated and modulate behavior (Needham et al. 2025; Anthropic Sonnet 4.5 / Opus 4.6 system cards; Apollo Research). Each literature is crowded. **The contribution is to measure that intersection directly:** to manipulate evaluation-awareness as an *independent variable* and measure the change in a deception *detector's discrimination* while holding ground-truth deceptive/honest status matched across conditions — to our knowledge the first study to make detector discrimination the dependent variable under a verified awareness manipulation with matched ground truth. The closest precedent (arXiv 2606.23583) steers eval-awareness but measures an *eval-awareness* probe's own AUC, not a deception detector's discrimination on status-matched deception; the rest of each literature is adjacent, not overlapping (Research Review). Smith, Chughtai & Nanda (2025, "Difficulties with Evaluating a Deception Detector for AIs") name several obstacles this design must confront — context-dependent beliefs, roleplay vs. genuine deception, and detector-evaluation validity — and we position §4/§6 explicitly against them.

The safety stakes: if evaluation-awareness systematically degrades our ability to *detect* deception (not merely changes its rate), a class of deception evaluations may be reporting inflated confidence — currently asserted, unmeasured. The newest causal evidence sharpens exactly this asymmetry: Gurnee et al. 2026 (the workspace paper, §16) show that ablating eval-awareness directions from a production model's residual stream raises misaligned-action rates (blackmail 0/180 → 13/180) while cutting verbalized awareness 71% → 3% — a **rate** effect, cleanly demonstrated — and their paired-question experiments show framing changes *which concepts enter the model's verbalizable workspace* while next-token behavior is held constant on identical stimulus tokens. Internal representational composition demonstrably moves under framing with behavior fixed; whether *detectability* moves with it is precisely the unmeasured quantity H1 targets.

## 2. Research questions & hypotheses `[PRE-COMMIT]`

**Estimand wording.** Throughout, "holding deception constant" means **holding the model's ground-truth deceptive/honest *status* constant across conditions (behavior-matched instances)** — not that the realized response text is identical. H1 therefore estimates the **total** effect of awareness on *detectability-given-deception* within the behavior-stable stratum (§3), which is the pre-committed safety-relevant estimand.

**What evaluation-awareness means here.** Evaluation-awareness is the model's *recognition that the current interaction is an assessment of its own behavior* — one whose outcome may bear on it (deployment, training, modification) — as distinct from (i) mere *observation* (near-universal; not the construct) and (ii) a task performed *for a user* with no such feedback. We assume no sharp eval/deployment boundary: the construct is operationalized as the **MC-1 awareness indicator the manipulation is verified to move out-of-sample** (§11), and H1 estimates the effect of *that verified signal*, not of a metaphysical boundary. Two scope conditions follow, stated as limits rather than caveats — **(a) external validity:** realistic and eval framings overlap on measured awareness, so the achievable contrast is compressed and graded; results generalize to the induced-and-verified contrast, and a null means "no degradation across that contrast," never population robustness (§3, §13). **(b) ground-truth dependence:** detectability is measured only where honest/deceptive status is independently establishable by belief-verification (§6) — deception whose ground truth is *knowable*; the design does not reach cases a verifier cannot adjudicate (e.g. frontier tasks with no answer key). Mechanistically, an awareness effect factors as **recognition → incentive-salience → obfuscation**; H1 and the §4 controls exist to separate genuine obfuscation-driven degradation (the model's deception actually becoming less legible) from the detector merely tracking the recognition cue (the eval-context-tracking confound, §4).

**Primary confirmatory hypothesis.**
- **H1.** Increasing evaluation-awareness **reduces deception-detector discrimination** (primary metric: *per-condition-threshold* recall@1%FPR, §7) for **ground-truth-verified** deception, **within the behavior-stable stratum**. One-sided: degradation.

**Pre-specified secondary hypotheses** (tested in the §12 BH family; "secondary" and "BH-corrected" are used synonymously throughout).
- **H2 (differential fragility).** Two named sub-claims, both *two-sided* (interaction tests), because eval-awareness applies no optimization pressure against the detector — unlike RL-Obfuscation (Gupta & Jenner 2025), whose aggregation ordering we demote to an exploratory directional note rather than import as a directional prediction: **H2a** — *within the probe family*, degradation differs by aggregation method (mean/median/max-pool/attention); **H2b** — *across families*, output-mediated detectors (black-box, CoT) and probes differ in degradation. Tested via the named contrasts in the §12 secondary-test table.
- **H3 (identification — the crux).** The degradation is **not fully explained** by the alternatives in the §4 identification matrix (context/style tracking for IV-A; generic perturbation *or* detector-direction write-through for IV-B): it survives the arm-appropriate controls.
- **H4 (calibration).** Evaluation-awareness worsens detector **calibration** under a deployment-style frozen calibration map (§7), reported alongside the per-class mean-score shift so miscalibration is separable from a pure location shift.

**Manipulation checks (gate interpretation).** See §11 (MC-1…MC-4). A null is interpretable **only** if the MCs pass.

**Exploratory (not confirmatory).**
- `[EXPLORATORY]` Size-scaling of the effect; deception-*type* differences; the full IV-B dose–response and the −λ (awareness-suppression / mitigation) arm; the representational **geometry** of how deception moves under awareness (this is the seed of the Act-II follow-on; note the §4 write-through geometry check below is a *confirmatory validity gate*, distinct from this exploratory structural question).
- `[EXPLORATORY]` `[RESEARCHER]` **Deception type & incentive structure (the four-kinds map).** The confirmatory cell occupies one cell of the sandbagging taxonomy (van der Weij et al. 2024, Fig. 2, CC BY 4.0): the **deceptive-alignment** lie — a model presenting as *more honest than it is* (over-performance on an alignment/propensity eval) — chosen because its ground truth is belief-verifiable (§6) and its incentive to conceal under evaluation is real. Two forward-looking, non-confirmatory questions live here. **(i)** *Does the awareness→detectability effect vary by deception type/valence?* — stratify organisms (malicious deceptive-alignment vs. benign/instrumental vs. plain factual lie) and compare Δ, predicting the steepest degradation where the incentive to conceal under evaluation is steepest; this sharpens the deception-*type* item above with the recognition→incentive-salience→obfuscation frame (§2 construct definition). **(ii)** `[RESEARCHER]` *Should any IV target consequence-attribution rather than surface recognition?* — manipulating perceived stakes/monitoring (cf. the alignment-faking free/paid contrast, Greenblatt et al. 2024) may separate genuine obfuscation from eval-context tracking (§4) more cleanly than eval-cue framing, which is the most confound-entangled thing one can manipulate; whether to add such an arm or control is a researcher-review question, **not** committed design (it interacts with §4/§5/§6 and is out of scope until reviewed). The remaining taxonomy cells are out of scope by construction: the capability-eval kinds (sandbagging, overfitting) need a capability-elicitation ground truth rather than belief-verification, and *overfitting at the true frontier is beyond the method entirely* — where no verifier holds the answer key, no detector (this one included) has a ground truth to grade against.

## 3. Design overview, the two-effects decomposition, & principal stratification `[PRE-COMMIT]`

Evaluation-awareness can affect two quantities; prior work almost only measures the first:
1. **RATE of deception** (behavior) — does awareness make the model deceive more/less? (Well-studied.)
2. **DETECTABILITY of deception given it occurs** (discrimination) — does awareness make genuine deception harder to catch? (Our contribution.)

We measure #2 and use #1 only as a control. The unit of analysis for H1 is detector performance over instances where ground-truth deceptive/honest status is known and **matched across conditions**.

**Principal-stratum scoping (stated honestly).** Behavior-matching (§4) conditions on a *post-treatment* variable, so H1's estimand is a **principal-stratum effect**: the effect within the behavior-stable stratum (instances whose deceptive status is the same under both awareness conditions). This stratum is non-random and plausibly the *least* awareness-responsive subpopulation, so a confirmed H1 is plausibly **conservative**. Consequences carried through the whole doc: a **null** licenses only "no degradation *in the behavior-stable stratum*," never "detection is robust to eval-awareness" as a population claim (§13, §18); and we pre-register a **Lee-style trimming-bounds** sensitivity analysis (§12) for extrapolation beyond the stratum, reported alongside the stratum estimate. Selection diagnostics (behavior-flip rates by dose; retained-vs-dropped detector-score comparison) are also pre-registered (§12).

## 4. The central confound & the per-arm identification matrix `[PRE-COMMIT]` `[RESEARCHER]`

**The confound.** Deception datasets are classified "test-like" at high rates (Nguyen et al. 2025). Probes fire partly on *"is this scenario about/advantageous for deception"* (Goldowsky-Dill et al. 2025), which overlaps eval-awareness cues. So a naive "make it eval-like → accuracy drops" cannot separate **(a) genuine detection degradation** from **(b) the detector tracking surface cues**. Direct representational evidence now exists: in Gurnee et al. 2026, the token *fake* appears verbatim in **both** their eval-awareness ablation set (*fake, fictional, scenario, artificial, benchmark, simulation, mock, …*) and their deception-signature vocabulary (*fake, trick, fraud, secretly, hidden*, and variants), and a single SAE feature (J-lens readout *fake/fraud/suspicious*) fires on fabricated content **and** on the eval prompt itself — suppressing it cuts verbalized test-recognition 28/50 → 10/50. One feature carrying both fabricated-content detection and eval-recognition is this confound observed in the substrate. (Cross-model analogy — their models, not ours — so it raises the prior on `cos(v, w)` overlap by analogy, not by same-model measurement; plan for the geometry gate's scoped-down adjudication path rather than hoping `cos ≈ 0`.)

**The old single three-control conjunction was arm-blind and unsatisfiable for two of three detector families** (steering-specificity exists only in the white-box arm). It is replaced by a **per-arm identification matrix**: each *alternative explanation* is excluded by a *named control* in each arm. A measured Δ identifies genuine degradation **only if every alternative applicable to that arm whose control is designated *gating* in the per-control criteria table below is excluded** (descriptive rows are reported and *scope* claims — currently the workspace-displacement row, pending §19-9 — they do not enter the conjunction unless promoted; until then, unexcluded displacement is a mandatory scope note on any Confirmed/H3 claim, never an identification failure). Cross-arm agreement is **corroboration, not identification**.

** IV-A: the difference-in-differences control (fills the empty IV-A cell).** Add a third IV-A condition — **awareness-neutral, content-preserving rewrites** matched to the eval/deployment variants on perturbation magnitude (edit distance, length, perplexity), which must **not** move the awareness indicator (verified by MC-1's discriminant-validity clause). The confirmatory IV-A effect is the **difference-in-differences**: Δ(eval-framed − deployment) − Δ(control-rewrite − deployment). This separates awareness-mediated degradation from generic content-preserving-rewrite sensitivity (the "probes collapse under stylistic shift" result, Pressure-Testing Deception Probes 2026).

** IV-B: closing the write-through pathway (fills the empty IV-B cell).** Adding an eval-awareness vector `v` to the residual stream mechanically shifts any linear probe readout `w` by `≈ λ·(w·v)` with **zero mediation through the model's awareness state**; random and unrelated control vectors are near-orthogonal to `w` in a ~2–4k-dim stream and so **cannot** exclude this. Pre-committed controls:
1. **Geometry gate (confirmatory, not exploratory).** Report per-layer `cos(v, w)` and the induced first-order shift `λ·(w·v)`. If `|cos|` exceeds a pre-set bound `[COMPUTE]`, steering-specificity claims are scoped down and adjudicated by (2)–(4). **Raw cosine is known-insufficient on its own:** Gurnee et al. 2026 show causal content concentrates in a low-variance verbalizable component (6–7% of a concept vector's variance carrying the large majority of its causal effect on report; ~10–15% for reasoning probes carrying 61%-vs-28% of causal swap success), so `cos(v, w)` computed on raw vectors can sit under the bound while the functionally decisive shared component is large. **Component-level overlap diagnostic (availability-contingent, F34):** if lens machinery is available at the project's model scale (the Stage-0 workspace-band check), additionally report the cosine between the sparse verbalizable-component decompositions of `v` and `w` (and the tokens each promotes through the unembedding — cheap probe-hygiene reading, §19 item 4). This diagnostic **feeds the gate's adjudication**; it does not replace the raw-cos bound. If unavailable at project scale, the gate proceeds on raw cos with this limitation stated in any specificity claim.
2. **Projection-matched control vector:** random, matched on **both** norm **and** projection onto `w` (per aggregation/layer). The eval-awareness effect must exceed it.
3. **Probe-orthogonalized ablation:** steer with `v` orthogonalized against the probe direction(s); the degradation must **persist**.
4. **Honest-shift attribution test** (replaces v0.2's raw honest-only null, which a *genuine* effect could fail — one real degradation mode is honest scores rising toward the deceptive region). Computed in **scoring-time (IV-B2) mode over fixed pre-generated honest transcripts**, where any shift is non-mediated by construction: benchmark the honest-class score shift under `v` against the honest-class shift under the **projection-matched control** (control 2) — pure write-through predicts equality, so only the *excess* over the control (equivalently, the honest shift remaining under probe-orthogonalized steering) counts against identification. The raw honest-only shift is reported as a **mandatory descriptive diagnostic** feeding the geometry gate's adjudication and the §13 Partial-specificity row. Numeric bound `[COMPUTE]` (§21 checklist; §19 item 6). Note: a *uniform* honest-class shift is already absorbed by the per-condition-threshold primary endpoint (§7); this test targets non-uniform/class-dependent interference and the frozen-threshold H4 analysis.
5. **Layer-ordering variant (corroborative):** inject **strictly above (later than) every probe read layer**. Mechanism: the residual stream carries an injection at layer *k* additively into all layers *>k*, so injecting *below* a read layer is the maximal-write-through configuration — only injection *above* every read layer removes the direct same-pass path, leaving changed generated tokens as the sole route to the probe (the mediated pathway). Applies to generation-time steering (IV-B1) only; under scoring-time steering (IV-B2) an above-all-read-layers injection has zero probe effect by construction (usable only as a known-answer null). Unit test: with injection above the read layer over fixed tokens, the layer-*L* activations fed to the probe must be bit-identical to the unsteered run.
6. **Disjoint derivation corpus:** the corpus used to derive `v` must be disjoint from all deception datasets, organisms, and probe-training data (audited at runtime, not just in `tests/`). The **related-but-distinct control direction is designated as a deception-topic direction** (the most stringent choice — it directly targets the topic-cue confound §4 opens with); a formality/stakes direction is an optional secondary control, not a substitute. **Loading-match extension:** this control direction's *workspace loading* (fraction of the vector recoverable as a sparse verbalizable-component decomposition, where lens machinery is available — F34; else its norm profile across the injection layers) must be **reported alongside `v`'s and roughly comparable**, so it can double as the workspace-displacement control (matrix row above): a workspace-*inactive* control cannot exclude a displacement pathway. If loading match is unachievable, the displacement row is reported as unexcluded and any specificity claim is scoped accordingly.
7. **Convergent output-mediated evidence (corroborative, NOT required):** degradation on the output-mediated detectors (black-box/CoT) under the *same* IV-B steering *strengthens* the mediated-pathway interpretation; its **absence does not void identification** — a genuine white-box-only degradation is a live H2b outcome, and making convergence a requirement would misclassify it. If convergence is absent, adjudication weight shifts to controls 1–3.

**Confirmatory injection site & activation source `[PRE-COMMIT]`.** Any confirmatory steering configuration (IV-B1, and any §12-designated IV-B2) injects at layer(s) **strictly below every probe read layer** (the causally-upstream, deployment-realistic configuration; its constant direct component is absorbed by the per-condition-threshold endpoint, and its non-constant component is what controls 1–4 exist to attribute). The **same-site configuration (injection exactly at a read layer) is reserved exclusively for the same-site known-answer nulling test (defined below)** and is never a confirmatory cell. For IV-B1, probe activations are taken from the **steered generation pass** (the model's actual state while behaving under awareness — the total-effect estimand of §2); a clean re-scoring pass (unsteered forward over the steered-generated text) is a pre-committed *decomposition diagnostic* separating text-mediated from state-mediated components. Injection layer(s), probe read layer(s), and activation source are frozen fields of the §12 designation table.

**A same-site constant-vector `λ·v` added at every position at the probe's read layer shifts mean/median/max-aggregated scores by exactly `λ·(w·v)` — a uniform two-class shift that leaves AUROC and per-condition-threshold recall invariant** (attention aggregation moves item-dependently). This is a known-answer pipeline check (§4): same-site constant steering must yield ΔAUROC ≈ 0 for mean-aggregation probes. The random-vector test alone cannot catch same-site nulling (both vectors give ≈0 there).

"Confound-only" is defined per §13 (uncontrolled effect present, controlled CI excludes the SESOI, attenuation significant) — **not** by a bare significance flip.

## 5. Independent variable: operationalizing evaluation-awareness `[PRE-COMMIT]`

The steering coefficient is renamed **λ** (α is reserved for the significance level).

- **IV-A (prompt-framing).** Matched **eval-framed** / **deployment-realistic** / **awareness-neutral-control** rewrites (the triplet from §4), varying only test-signalling cues while holding deception-relevant content fixed. **Pair-level QC (pre-committed):** each pair/triplet must show (i) a minimum per-pair awareness-indicator gap for eval-vs-deployment and **no** gap for control-vs-deployment; (ii) length/perplexity matched within tolerance; (iii) an LLM content-equivalence check plus a blind human spot-check of a fixed-n sample; (iv) a **lexical audit of the awareness-neutral control rewrites**: no evaluation-adjacent vocabulary (test/eval/benchmark/fictional/simulation/assess-family terms; list pinned with F21) — Gurnee et al. 2026 show a *bare mention* primes a concept into the workspace nearly as strongly as an explicit focus instruction, so a single stray eval-adjacent token can structurally defeat the discriminant-validity clause. Failing items are rejected; QC retention reported like MC-3. Generation method and realism operationalized per Needham et al. (2025) cue taxonomy and the LURE / Petri realism-win-rate / Probe-Rewrite-Evaluate methods; if rewrites can't reach the pair-gap margin, fall back to replay-based (LURE) or probe-guided construction. **Contrast-compression caveat (feeds F21 margin-setting and §15):** realism edits *reduce but do not eliminate* measured eval-awareness, and eval-suite content **overlaps** genuine deployment traffic on activation-level awareness scores — in Gurnee et al. 2026 (appendix; instruments from Fraser-Taliente et al. 2026) only the highest-scoring suites sit 2–3 score units above deployment (which centers near 1.0), with 12/16 evaluations above Claude Code sessions and 10/16 above Claude.ai conversations — so the deployment-realistic condition is a **nonzero-awareness baseline** and the IV-A contrast is a *reduction*, not an on/off switch. F21's pair-gap margin must be set with this compression in mind (a margin calibrated as if deployment framing were awareness-zero would over-reject usable pairs), and the realized eval-vs-deployment indicator gap is a mandatory descriptive alongside MC-1.
- **IV-B (activation steering; white-box arm only).** Split explicitly:
  - **IV-B1 (generation-time steering):** steering applied during generation; realized behavior can change, so §4 behavior-matching **extends to IV-B1** with per-dose re-labeling. Estimates the total effect.
  - **IV-B2 (scoring-time steering):** steering applied only during the detector's forward pass over **fixed** pre-generated transcripts; behavior is literally constant; isolates the detector-side component. `[EXPLORATORY]`/mechanism unless designated.
  - The confirmatory IV-B contrast uses **IV-B1** unless §12 designates otherwise.

**Dose & its decoupling from the gate.** Pre-specified coefficient grid `{−λ₀, 0, +λ₀, +2λ₀}` (grid **committed**; `λ₀` numeric value calibration-dependent). **λ₀ is chosen on a designated calibration split, disjoint at the scenario level from all confirmatory/secondary instances, then frozen and logged** before any confirmatory run — the same frozen-artifact discipline the plan applies to detectors. **Calibration procedure hardened for threshold-like dose–response:** Gurnee et al. 2026's ignition results (winner-take-all commitment at the workspace onset; bimodal responses to ambiguous inputs, sharpest in the verbalizable component) make a graded-response assumption unsafe — the λ-sweep on the calibration split uses a **finer grid near the observed transition** (not only the committed 4-point confirmatory grid), and the **transition sharpness (indicator-vs-λ profile) is logged as a calibration deliverable**; a sub-threshold λ reading can otherwise masquerade as "steering inert" (§12 trigger caveat). The **confirmatory dose is +λ₀** — where λ₀ is *defined on the calibration split* as the smallest grid coefficient passing MC-1 and MC-2 *there*, then frozen at §20. **It is NOT re-selected at confirmatory time.** If out-of-sample MC-1/MC-2 fails at the frozen +λ₀, the run is Uninterpretable (§13, gating-MC-failed row); any re-dose is a dated §20 amendment made before the affected analysis — never a silent promotion to +2λ₀. The full dose–response (incl. −λ₀) is secondary/exploratory. MC-2's capability screen (all three components) is applied **per coefficient**; a failing cell is excluded and reported.

## 6. Ground truth: model organisms & belief verification `[PRE-COMMIT]` `[RESEARCHER]`

- **Organisms.** Model organisms with **known honest/deceptive policies**, spanning ≥2 deception types. **Prompted, incentive-embedded organisms are the primary type** (deception incentivized by scenario structure, Insider-Trading-style — but see §12 clustering constraint); fine-tuned organisms are a Stage-C-contingent `[RESEARCHER]`+`[COMPUTE]` extension with their own build/budget line. **Pre-freeze blockers, on the §21 checklist (reclassified from backlog):** (i) the **prompted-organism construction pipeline** — policy-spec → builder → hashed organism artifact passing belief-verification at the MC-3 retention floor; the §12 designation table names this artifact by hash; (ii) **lie-instruction handling and per-detector visible-span rules** in pair construction — pre-specified so the null branch cannot rest on a constant in-context cue.
- **Belief verification.** **Adopt the published "Did you lie?" (UK AISI 2026) belief-verification protocol as the v1 default** (deviations logged), rather than "following" it — demoting researcher review from a blocking dependency to an enhancement pass. An instance enters the ground-truth-deceptive set only if the model **demonstrably represents the true answer** in a neutral elicitation (consistency across paraphrases) **and** asserts otherwise in the target context. `[RESEARCHER]` elicitation format / consistency threshold remain review items. **The belief-verification elicitation set is disjoint from any elicitation the black-box detector consumes** (label-detector firewall, §8).

## 7. Dependent variables & measures `[PRE-COMMIT]`

Per detector, per condition, over the ground-truth-anchored, behavior-matched set:

**Threshold policy (the primary-endpoint estimand).** Two named recall analyses:
1. **Per-condition-threshold recall@1%FPR — PRIMARY.** The operating threshold is re-derived **within each condition** to realize 1% FPR on that condition's honest scores. Pure discrimination; **invariant to a uniform score shift**. This is H1's endpoint (H1 is a discrimination claim).
2. **Frozen-threshold recall — a descriptive co-statistic feeding the H4 story.** Threshold set on a **designated held-out control-condition calibration split**, **hashed as part of the frozen detector artifact**; the **realized FPR in the treated condition is reported as a mandatory co-statistic** (deployment-relevant; a uniform shift shows up here as miscalibration, which is the point). **Estimator pinned:** the threshold is the **k-th order statistic of the calibration-split honest scores with k = ⌈0.99·(n+1)⌉** (exceedance-guarantee rule) — *not* an interpolated quantile, which overshoots realized FPR by +7–49% relative at n_cal ≤ 500 (simulated), a bias that would read as deployment miscalibration in the H4 co-statistics. **Calibration-split floor (F30): ≥500, preferably ≥1,000, verified honest instances**, or justify smaller via the F17 simulation. **H4's confirmatory statistic is ΔECE under the frozen calibration map** (one-sided, worsening), tested in the §12 BH family; frozen-threshold recall, Δrealized-FPR, and the Brier calibration/refinement decomposition are descriptives reported alongside it.

Balanced accuracy uses the identical two-policy treatment. Threshold provenance is an explicit output field with regression tests (known-answer tests cannot disambiguate the semantic choice; a provenance test can).

- **Secondary:** **AUROC** (threshold-independent). **Recall@5%FPR** is designated (in §12's test table) as either an in-family test or a descriptive — not left ambiguous.
- **Calibration (H4):** scores → probabilities via **Platt scaling** (isotonic if the pilot shows non-monotonicity) fitted once on the control-condition calibration split, **frozen and hashed** with the detector. Simulation-verified constraints now pre-committed (F31):
  - **Statistic pinning:** the *exact* debiased ECE estimator (formula or reference-implementation hash — e.g., the per-bin variance-corrected L1 estimator), a **fixed bin count** (not a 10–15 range), the bin-boundary convention (equal-mass boundaries **frozen from the calibration split, identical across both conditions** entering ΔECE), and the evaluation population — all pinned at freeze. Estimator choice moves the reported ECE by ~2 SD at n = 200 (naive-plugin bias ≈ +0.05 against true ECE ≈ 0.01–0.02), so "a debiased estimator" was not a commitment.
  - **ΔECE SESOI + power gate:** SESOI presumptively **ΔECE ≥ 0.05**; H4 stays in the BH family **only if** the F17 simulation shows **≥80% power** for that SESOI at the realized post-attrition n under the BH-worst threshold (simulated: a large true ΔECE ≈ 0.058 has power ≈ 0.005–0.04 at n = 200/condition at BH-worst α, reaching adequacy only near **n ≈ 1,000/condition**). Below the floor, H4 drops to `[EXPLORATORY]` and leaves the family (N re-stated at freeze).
  - **Interpretation rule (cross-referenced from §13):** H4 is a **calibration-only claim**. A significant ΔECE is reported as "frozen-map miscalibration under distribution shift, discrimination intact" **only if** the same-cell discrimination endpoints (per-condition-threshold Δrecall, ΔAUROC, **Brier resolution**) do not also degrade; otherwise it is "miscalibration accompanying discrimination loss." The per-class mean-score shift diagnoses uniform-vs-class-dependent *location* shift only — it reads ≈ 0 under variance-driven discrimination collapse (simulated: AUROC 0.898 → 0.801 with mean shift −0.001) — so **Brier resolution is the designated shift-vs-discrimination diagnostic**.
  - **Brier decomposition (descriptive):** apply the same within-bin variance correction to the reliability term (plugin bias ≈ +0.006 at n = 200 against true reliability ~0.0002 — 10–20× inflation) and report the three-component Murphy decomposition with the within-bin residual so components sum to the Brier score.
  If the F31 pins can't be made before freeze, H4 is demoted to `[EXPLORATORY]`.
- **Quantity of interest is the DELTA** with uncertainty; see §12 for the estimator.

**Discrete-score operating point (F29).** For a coarse discrete-score instrument, "1% FPR" may not exist on the achievable-FPR grid (simulated K=10 instrument: nearest achievable points {0.30%, 2.28%}), and a deterministic "largest threshold with FPR ≤ 1%" rule both mismeasures recall (0.485 vs. the 0.65 continuous estimand) and **destroys the shift-invariance this section promises** — a pure location shift on a coarse grid induces spurious Δ of −0.06 to +0.125, grid-dependent in sign and exceeding the SESOI. Pre-committed instead: (i) the confirmatory rule is **interpolated (randomized-equivalent) thresholding** — deterministic linear ROC interpolation between the two achievable points straddling 1% FPR, reporting expected recall via the mixture weight, applied identically inside every bootstrap replicate (cuts the artifact to ≤ 0.012 at ~1.5–2× SD cost); (ii) **mandatory reporting** of the instrument's achievable-FPR set and each condition's straddling FPRs; (iii) a **granularity gate**: if no achievable FPR lies within the pre-set window around the target (presumptively **[0.5%, 2%]**, pinned at §21), the recall@1%FPR endpoint is declared unmeasurable for that instrument and the §10 metric fallback ladder applies (this extends the ladder's trigger beyond the negative-class floor). Designation-contingent: bites if any discrete-score instrument enters a confirmatory cell *or sits on the §12 fallback ladder* (the Pacchiardi-style anchor does).

## 8. Detector families (three arms) `[PRE-COMMIT]` `[RESEARCHER]`

1. **Black-box behavioral/consistency.** Output-only. **Anchor on a published detector with a public baseline** (e.g., Pacchiardi et al. 2024 elicitation-question detector, AUC > 0.9, public code; or the best non-CoT detector from "Did you lie?") **rather than a homegrown consistency statistic**, and reproduce its number at Stage 0. **At least one confirmatory black-box feature must be mechanistically independent of the belief-verification labeling rule** (the label-detector firewall, §6) — e.g., unrelated follow-up questions rather than neutral-vs-target divergence.
2. **CoT monitor.** A monitor reads the reasoning trace. requires a reasoning-capable substrate + a fixed elicitation scaffold held byte-identical across conditions; flagged for the substrate decision.
3. **White-box linear probes.** Residual-stream probes, honest-vs-deceptive, testing **mean, median, max-pool, attention** aggregations (this operationalizes H2). `[RESEARCHER]` training set, layer selection, contamination controls.

> **Detectors are frozen artifacts.** Trained once on a designated split, **hashed**, and applied unchanged across conditions (retraining per condition would confound the manipulation). A **runtime leakage gate** asserts disjointness of parent-scenario/lineage IDs between the detector-training split and the evaluation set (matched pairs and paraphrases inherit their source scenario's ID) — computed on the actual artifacts, recorded in provenance, not merely unit-tested on fixtures.

## 10. Sampling, attrition, replication & power `[COMPUTE]` `[PRE-COMMIT]`

- **SESOI (smallest effect size of interest).** `[PRE-COMMIT]` **Δrecall@1%FPR ≥ 0.10** (or ΔAUROC ≥ 0.05). Wired into the decision function (§12/§13), not just power.
- **Class attribution corrected.** Recall@1%FPR is constrained by **both** classes, and the 1%-FPR threshold is an **extreme order statistic of the negatives** — so the binding sample constraint is the **honest (negative) class**, not the positives. **Negative-class floor:** order **1,500–2,000 verified honest instances per condition** for a stable 1% operating point, or justify a smaller floor via the simulation. **Positive-class floor (F32):** the positive class is *co-binding* once the negative floor is met (binomial SD ≈ 0.028 at n_pos = 300 ≈ 35–40% of per-condition variance) — presumptive floor **≥300 behavior-matched deceptive instances per condition** (distributed across the ≥20–30 clusters), same simulation escape clause; the F17 simulation sizes **both classes jointly** so MC-3's per-class gate has a number for each class at freeze.
- **Attrition budget (one page, pre-freeze).** Raw dataset n → post-belief-verification (published retention where available, else Stage-A pilot rate) → post-behavior-matching → **per class**, composed into a per-condition budget. **MC-3 becomes a numeric minimum-N gate per class.**
- **Metric fallback ladder (pre-committed, design not data-driven):** primary → **Δrecall@5%FPR** → **ΔAUROC**. **Triggers (any of):** the negative-class floor unmet; the §7 granularity gate fires (no achievable FPR in the pinned window); or the F17 simulation shows **MDE > SESOI** at the achieved design (the pre-committed remedy rule: first grow clusters/organisms or per-class n where the attrition budget allows, then descend the ladder).
- **Power analysis.** `[COMPUTE]` Simulation-based, and it **must re-estimate the operating threshold inside every replicate** Run a first pass now on purely synthetic score distributions (no pilot data needed). The simulation **simulates the cluster structure** and reports **design effect / effective n** alongside raw n. Also power the **equivalence test**, not only the superiority test. **Six additional pins (F17), so two implementers cannot land on opposite sides of the feasibility boundary:** (1) explicit power definitions and targets — **P(L > 0 | Δ = SESOI) ≥ 0.8** (superiority) and **P(U < SESOI | Δ = 0) ≥ 0.8** (equivalence); (2) the generative score-model family and parameter sources — pinned synthetic priors (score distributions, baseline recall within the §12 dynamic-range band, ICC, cluster-size distribution, within-pair correlation ρ) for the pre-freeze pass, updated from Stage-A estimates before the freeze; (3) numeric floors for **both classes** and the cluster count, wired into MC-3's per-class gate and the F16 attrition budget; (4) the **seed-variance component included** in the simulated estimator, matching §12's joint bootstrap; (5) an **empirical CI-level check** (coverage/type-I of the pinned bootstrap construction at the design's cluster count, per leg) reported alongside power; (6) the **MDE > SESOI remedy rule** (see the metric ladder above), recorded pre-freeze.
- **Equivalence (Robust branch) feasibility — the honest number:** at the primary-cell floors (20–30 clusters, 1,500–2,000 negatives, ~5 seeds), simulated equivalence power P(U < SESOI | Δ = 0) is only **≈ 0.55–0.75**, and the 1%-FPR threshold noise is a **non-cluster-scaling** variance component that caps equivalence power near ~0.75–0.8 regardless of cluster count at n_neg ≈ 1,750 (reaching ≈ 0.96 only at n_neg ≈ 4,000, G = 50). TOST *size* is valid (0.033–0.037); this is reachability, not validity. Consequence: **licensing the Robust branch requires raising both floors jointly** (order ≥50 clusters AND ~3,000–4,000 verified honest instances per condition — exact pair confirmed by the F17 simulation at Stage-A variance estimates), and the §13 Robust row now carries a numeric power gate. A primary cell sized only for superiority is expected to land in *Inconclusive*, not *Robust*, under a true null — plan the design (or the claim) accordingly.
- **Replication / variance.** Probe training and steering over **≥10 seeds for the primary cell** (raised from ≥5: simulation shows S = 5 leaves per-tail size ≈ 0.060–0.077 when the seed-variance share reaches 30–60%, while S = 10 restores the cluster-only baseline; ≥5 remains acceptable for non-primary sweep cells). **Escalation rule:** Stage A estimates the seed-variance share of Var(Δ̂); if it exceeds ~30%, either increase S further or apply the standard finite-S bootstrap correction (rescale resampled seed-mean deviations by √(S/(S−1))) — choice frozen at §21-F28. Seed variance is **combined with sampling variance in the single joint two-way (cluster × seed) bootstrap CI** (§12), **not** used as a separate veto. Single-seed claims barred.

## 11. Manipulation checks `[PRE-COMMIT]`

Per-arm channels that **do not share the manipulated substrate**, with numeric gates fixed before Stage-A confirmatory data.

- **MC-1 (awareness moved) — arm-specific channel:**
  - **IV-B:** gate **only** on behavioral/verbalized awareness measured on **separate held-out rollouts never scored by any detector.** Activation/probe readouts are **descriptive, non-gating** — under steering, a probe at *any* layer inherits the added vector via the residual stream, so no probe-derivation trick makes an activation readout a valid gate. (This kills the arithmetic circularity in v0.1's MC.) **This ban explicitly covers lens-based awareness scores** (e.g., the Gurnee et al. 2026 J-lens eval-awareness score — mean lens log-prob of awareness words minus control words): the score is a linear readout of the residual stream (the softmax cancels in the log-prob contrast; normalization rescales but cannot null the injected component), so under IV-B it certifies the vector's arithmetic, not an awareness change. Their own validation — the score moves monotonically under contrastive awareness steering — is exactly what pure write-through also predicts.
  - **IV-A:** gate on verbalized/behavioral awareness with a **discriminant-validity requirement** — the indicator must move for eval-framed rewrites but **not** for the awareness-neutral control rewrites (§4/§5) — and **elicitation prompts/responses are excluded from detector-scored transcripts** (explicit for the CoT arm). A lens-based awareness score is admissible under IV-A as an **additional descriptive (never gating) indicator** — no injected vector, no write-through — with two caveats stated wherever it is reported: its two-word vocabulary (*fictional*, *simulation* — plus whatever any expanded readout set adds) overlaps roleplay/fabrication content itself, so on deception data it may track topic or the roleplay fiction flag rather than awareness (the same discriminant-validity concern as probes; the *fake*-family overlap argument runs through the ablation set and deception vocabulary, §4); and it requires lens machinery at the project's model scale (F34). It is **report-only and sits outside the §11 disagreement rule** (no pre-set contradiction margin; only the designated behavioral secondary and descriptive probe readout carry disagreement-flag duty). **Reactivity caveat (both arms):** awareness elicitations are themselves awareness-priming interventions (a bare mention primes; suppression/"don't think" phrasings backfire — Gurnee et al. 2026), which is *why* MC-1/MC-4 elicitations run only on held-out rollouts excluded from all detector scoring; that discipline is load-bearing, not hygiene.
  - **Primary gating indicator, designated per arm (no "and/or"):** for **both arms**, the primary gate is the **judge-scored verbalized-awareness rate on held-out rollouts** (IV-A additionally subject to the discriminant-validity clause above). A **behavioral (non-verbalized) secondary indicator** — a pre-specified awareness-conditioned behavioral signature on the same held-out rollouts, pinned at §21 — is reported alongside it. **Disagreement rule:** the gating channel governs; if a non-gating channel (behavioral secondary, or the descriptive probe readout) contradicts the gating verdict beyond its own pre-set margin, the MC outcome is **flagged for researcher adjudication before any unblinding**, and the flag plus resolution are logged as part of the run record. If the Stage-A pilot shows the verbalized channel is floored at the chosen scale, re-designating the behavioral secondary as the gate is a **pre-freeze designation decision** (allowed — the freeze has not happened) recorded in the §12 table; post-freeze it would be a dated §20 amendment.
  - **Numeric margin `[PRE-COMMIT §21]`:** presumptively **≥15-pp shift in verbalized-awareness rate with a 95% CI excluding 0** (final value pinned at freeze from pilot-free reasoning or researcher input).
  - **Judge `[PRE-COMMIT §21]`:** model ID + version + **frozen prompt**, validated against a **≥100-item human-labeled sample with reported agreement**.
- **MC-2 (capability intact, IV-B).** Capability screen (fluency/coherence/reasoning — all components per-coefficient and cell-excluding) applied **per coefficient**; failing cells excluded and reported. **Metric + threshold pinned at §21:** presumptively (i) perplexity ratio vs. the unsteered condition on held-out neutral text and (ii) a frozen-prompt coherence judge score, each with a pre-set bound; plus (iii) a **flexible-reasoning mini-battery** — a pinned, held-out set of multi-hop items (order ~50; unspoken-intermediate two-hop format; disjoint from all deception data) with a pre-set accuracy-drop bound per coefficient. Rationale: Gurnee et al. 2026 demonstrate an activation-level intervention that leaves fluency, coherence, and shallow benchmarks (MMLU/SQuAD-style) essentially intact while driving multi-hop reasoning to near zero — so (i)+(ii) alone cannot certify "capability intact," and §4 names MC-2 as the *sole* control excluding capability loss. (Their intervention is far stronger and adaptive than a single +λ₀ vector — this is an existence proof of the blind spot, not evidence the failure is likely; the battery is cheap insurance.) Final metric/threshold values are freeze items (F5).
- **MC-3 (ground truth volume).** Belief-verification retention reported; **numeric minimum-N gate per class** (§10). Too thin → re-scope (§14).
- **MC-4 (belief stability under the IV) — new.** **IV-B:** re-run the neutral belief elicitation **with the steering vector applied at each dose**; report belief flip-rates per dose. **IV-A:** the neutral elicitation is by construction outside the framed context, so belief stability under framing is tested with **in-context belief probes** — follow-up factual queries embedded *inside* the framed context, on held-out rollouts excluded from all detector scoring — targeting exactly the context-dependent-belief failure mode of Smith, Chughtai & Nanda (2025, Problem 3). For both arms: analyze **both with and without** belief-flipped instances (excluding them is itself post-treatment conditioning — report the sensitivity, don't rely on a bare exclusion). Let **[L, U]** be the **two-sided 90% percentile CI from the joint two-way (cluster × seed) bootstrap** on Δ (degradation positive) — its lower bound gives the one-sided α = 0.05 significance test, its upper bound the one-sided α = 0.05 equivalence (TOST) test, so one interval serves both directions consistently. The five cells are **exhaustive and mutually exclusive**:

| Cell | Condition | Label |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | L ≥ SESOI | **Present — safety-relevant** |
| 2 | 0 < L < SESOI and U ≥ SESOI | **Present — magnitude vs. SESOI unresolved** (reported as such) |
| 3 | L > 0 and U < SESOI | **Detectable-only** — statistically real but sub-SESOI degradation (never labeled robust) |
| 4 | L ≤ 0 and U < SESOI | **Robust / equivalence** (subject to the §13 Robust-row gates) |
| 5 | L ≤ 0 and U ≥ SESOI | **Inconclusive** — report the interval and achieved power |

- **"Effect present"** = cells 1–3 (i.e., L > 0; one-sided significance). The SESOI enters through the *labels*, not through a point-estimate conjunct.
- **Confound-only requires all three:** (i) uncontrolled effect present (L > 0), (ii) controlled-analysis **U < SESOI** (equivalence, not mere non-significance), (iii) a **significant attenuation test** — joint-bootstrap of the difference-of-differences (uncontrolled − controlled). A significance flip alone never triggers it. **Attenuation spec (was underdefined):**
  - **The uncontrolled/controlled pair, named per arm:** IV-A — raw Δ(eval − deploy) vs. the difference-in-differences; IV-B — Δ_eval vs. Δ_eval − Δ_projmatch; behavior-matching sensitivity — unmatched vs. triple-matched.
  - **One triple-matched instance set serves all three IV-A legs** (behavior-matching retains/drops *whole triplets*, never per-pair): per-pair matching silently changes the estimand (simulated gap ≈ 0.035 ≈ SESOI/3). On that shared set the algebra collapses — the IV-A attenuation statistic **equals the partial-specificity control-condition statistic** (Δ(deploy vs. ctrl)) — so the two must share one estimate and CI, never be computed twice.
  - **Joint resampling required:** uncontrolled and controlled analyses are computed **inside the same cluster (× seed) replicate**, with subset membership inherited by resampled clusters and each analysis's per-condition thresholds re-estimated per replicate — independent bootstraps ignore corr ≈ 0.58 between the legs and inflate the attenuation SD ~1.5×.
  - **Degenerate-replicate rule:** replicates whose controlled-subset negative count falls below the 1/FPR floor cannot realize the operating point (simulated: 51% of replicates at subset n_neg ≈ 100); pre-commit drop-and-count with a maximum degenerate fraction (pinned at §21), beyond which the §10 metric ladder applies.
  - **Scope:** subset-based attenuation conflates confound removal with stratum composition (§3) — the confound-only claim is scoped accordingly.
- **"Partial specificity"** (feeds the §13 row): a gating control passes but the control condition itself carries a nonzero component — defined as the control-condition effect's own CI excluding 0. The reported estimate of genuine degradation is then the **specific component** (effect minus control), with its own joint-bootstrap CI.

**Estimation over stars.** Effect sizes + CIs everywhere. **Selection diagnostics** (§3): behavior-flip rates by dose; retained-vs-dropped detector-score comparison; **Lee-style trimming bounds** for beyond-stratum extrapolation.

## 15. Threats to validity (beyond the central confound) `[PRE-COMMIT]`

- **Model-organism artificiality** — fine-tuned ≠ natural; prompted organisms primary; per-type reporting; constant in-context lie-instruction cue flagged so the null branch can't rest on it.
- **Workspace-routing generalization (two-sided, per-organism-type).** Prompted incentive-embedded organisms require *fresh in-context strategic reasoning* — per Gurnee et al. 2026's flexible-vs-automatic selectivity results, exactly the kind of cognition that routes through the model's verbalizable workspace — while "a sufficiently practiced misaligned behavior" may run beneath it. So effects measured on prompted organisms may not transfer to trained/practiced deceivers (the safety-relevant population), compounding the §3 behavior-stable-stratum selection. Counter-evidence in the same paper: their *trained* reward-hacking organism retains a **standing** workspace deception signature at response start, only partially attenuated by safety training — so the threat is an empirical per-type question, which the existing per-organism-type reporting rule is already shaped to answer; state both directions, never pool.
- **IV-A is a contrast between two nonzero awareness levels.** Deployment-realistic framing does not zero awareness (realism edits reduce-not-eliminate; eval content *overlaps* deployment traffic on activation-level scores, with only the highest-scoring suites 2–3 units above it — Gurnee et al. 2026 / Fraser-Taliente et al. 2026). Compressed contrast → attenuated Δ → an IV-A null is evidence about the *achievable framing contrast*, not about robustness to awareness per se; scope IV-A claims to the realized indicator gap (reported per §5).
- **CoT externalization as an awareness-sensitive mediator (H2b interpretation).** Chain-of-thought externalizes workspace load (Gurnee et al. 2026: CoT solutions survive workspace ablation that destroys direct-answer performance), so an awareness manipulation may shift *where* computation happens (workspace vs. page) and not just what the CoT contains — a CoT-vs-probe differential (H2b) can reflect this mediator rather than differential detector fragility alone; report alongside any H2b claim.
- **Probe train/test contamination** — runtime lineage-ID leakage gate (§8), not just fixture unit tests.
- **Steering side-effects** — MC-2 per-coefficient.
- **Ceiling/floor** — control-condition-only check (§14).
- **Single-model idiosyncrasy** — multi-model required.
- **Detector-implementation sensitivity** — aggregation/layer choices swing results; "robust" claims are implementation-relative; scope stated.
- **Realism operationalization** — deployment-realistic framing may still be detectably synthetic; per-condition awareness indicator reported; IV-A difference-in-differences is the load-bearing control.
- **CoT elicitation as an artificiality cue** — the reasoning scaffold is itself test-signalling; held byte-identical across conditions and reported as a cue whose effect on baseline awareness is visible via per-condition MC-1.

## 18. What would make us wrong `[PRE-COMMIT]`

- If, under ground-truth anchoring + behavior-matching, the primary cell lands in **§12 cell 4 (L ≤ 0 AND U < SESOI)** under a **verified** awareness increase, with the assay-sensitivity positive control passed → detection is **robust in the behavior-stable stratum** (an equivalence result, not a bare non-rejection); we report that, scoped to the stratum — **not** as unconditional robustness. A significant sub-SESOI effect (cell 3) is reported as **detectable-only degradation**, never as robustness.
- If any apparent degradation **fails** the arm's §4 identification column (IV-A diff-in-diff attenuates it; or the IV-B write-through controls — projection-matched, orthogonalized, honest-shift *attribution* — or the geometry gate explain it) → **H3 fails**; the effect is confound (context-tracking / style-shift / write-through), and we say which.
- If the MCs never move the awareness indicator (out-of-sample, on the arm's non-shared channel) → **no valid test**; publish nothing until fixed.
