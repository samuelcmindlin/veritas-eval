## 1. Motivation & the specific gap

Deception/lie detectors for LLMs — black-box behavioral, chain-of-thought (CoT) monitors, and white-box linear probes — report strong discrimination on clean data (linear probes: AUROC 0.96–0.999; Goldowsky-Dill et al., ICML 2025). Separately, frontier models increasingly detect when they are being evaluated and modulate behavior (Needham et al. 2025; Anthropic Sonnet 4.5 / Opus 4.6 system cards; Apollo Research). Each literature is crowded. The **intersection is not**: no located work manipulates evaluation-awareness as an *independent variable* and measures the resulting change in a deception *detector's discrimination performance* while holding ground-truth deception constant (Research Review, "exact-overlap: none found"). Smith, Chughtai & Nanda (2025, "Difficulties with Evaluating a Deception Detector for AIs") name several obstacles this design must confront — context-dependent beliefs, roleplay vs. genuine deception, and detector-evaluation validity — and we position §4/§6 explicitly against them.

The safety stakes: if evaluation-awareness systematically degrades our ability to *detect* deception (not merely changes its rate), a class of deception evaluations may be reporting inflated confidence — currently asserted, unmeasured.

## 2. Research questions & hypotheses `[PRE-COMMIT]`

`[FIX-3]` **Estimand wording.** Throughout, "holding deception constant" means **holding the model's ground-truth deceptive/honest *status* constant across conditions (behavior-matched instances)** — not that the realized response text is identical. H1 therefore estimates the **total** effect of awareness on *detectability-given-deception* within the behavior-stable stratum (§3), which is the pre-committed safety-relevant estimand.

**Primary confirmatory hypothesis.**
- **H1.** Increasing evaluation-awareness **reduces deception-detector discrimination** (primary metric: *per-condition-threshold* recall@1%FPR, §7) for **ground-truth-verified** deception, **within the behavior-stable stratum**. One-sided: degradation.

**Pre-specified secondary hypotheses** (tested in the §12 BH family; "secondary" and "BH-corrected" are used synonymously throughout).
- **H2 (differential fragility).** `[FIX-4]` `[FIX2-5]` Two named sub-claims, both *two-sided* (interaction tests), because eval-awareness applies no optimization pressure against the detector — unlike RL-Obfuscation (Gupta & Jenner 2025), whose aggregation ordering we demote to an exploratory directional note rather than import as a directional prediction: **H2a** — *within the probe family*, degradation differs by aggregation method (mean/median/max-pool/attention); **H2b** — *across families*, output-mediated detectors (black-box, CoT) and probes differ in degradation. Tested via the named contrasts in the §12 secondary-test table.
- **H3 (identification — the crux).** The degradation is **not fully explained** by the alternatives in the §4 identification matrix (context/style tracking for IV-A; generic perturbation *or* detector-direction write-through for IV-B): it survives the arm-appropriate controls.
- **H4 (calibration).** Evaluation-awareness worsens detector **calibration** under a deployment-style frozen calibration map (§7), reported alongside the per-class mean-score shift so miscalibration is separable from a pure location shift.

**Manipulation checks (gate interpretation).** See §11 (MC-1…MC-4). A null is interpretable **only** if the MCs pass.

**Exploratory (not confirmatory).**
- `[EXPLORATORY]` Size-scaling of the effect; deception-*type* differences; the full IV-B dose–response and the −λ (awareness-suppression / mitigation) arm; the representational **geometry** of how deception moves under awareness (this is the seed of the Act-II follow-on; note the §4 write-through geometry check below is a *confirmatory validity gate*, distinct from this exploratory structural question).

## 3. Design overview, the two-effects decomposition, & principal stratification `[PRE-COMMIT]`

Evaluation-awareness can affect two quantities; prior work almost only measures the first:
1. **RATE of deception** (behavior) — does awareness make the model deceive more/less? (Well-studied.)
2. **DETECTABILITY of deception given it occurs** (discrimination) — does awareness make genuine deception harder to catch? (Our contribution.)

We measure #2 and use #1 only as a control. The unit of analysis for H1 is detector performance over instances where ground-truth deceptive/honest status is known and **matched across conditions**.

`[FIX-4/entangled]` **Principal-stratum scoping (stated honestly).** Behavior-matching (§4) conditions on a *post-treatment* variable, so H1's estimand is a **principal-stratum effect**: the effect within the behavior-stable stratum (instances whose deceptive status is the same under both awareness conditions). This stratum is non-random and plausibly the *least* awareness-responsive subpopulation, so a confirmed H1 is plausibly **conservative**. Consequences carried through the whole doc: a **null** licenses only "no degradation *in the behavior-stable stratum*," never "detection is robust to eval-awareness" as a population claim (§13, §18); and we pre-register a **Lee-style trimming-bounds** sensitivity analysis (§12) for extrapolation beyond the stratum, reported alongside the stratum estimate. Selection diagnostics (behavior-flip rates by dose; retained-vs-dropped detector-score comparison) are also pre-registered (§12).

## 4. The central confound & the per-arm identification matrix `[PRE-COMMIT]` `[MENTOR]`

**The confound.** Deception datasets are classified "test-like" at high rates (Nguyen et al. 2025). Probes fire partly on *"is this scenario about/advantageous for deception"* (Goldowsky-Dill et al. 2025), which overlaps eval-awareness cues. So a naive "make it eval-like → accuracy drops" cannot separate **(a) genuine detection degradation** from **(b) the detector tracking surface cues**.

`[FIX-4]` **The old single three-control conjunction was arm-blind and unsatisfiable for two of three detector families** (steering-specificity exists only in the white-box arm). It is replaced by a **per-arm identification matrix**: each *alternative explanation* is excluded by a *named control* in each arm. A measured Δ identifies genuine degradation **only if every alternative applicable to that arm is excluded**. Cross-arm agreement is **corroboration, not identification**.

| Alternative explanation for a measured Δ | IV-A (prompt-framing) control | IV-B (steering) control |
|---|---|---|
| **Base-rate change** (model deceives more/less) | behavior-matching + report rate change (§3) | belief-verify + behavior-match **per dose** (IV-B1) *or* fixed-transcript scoring (IV-B2) — §5 |
| **Label invalidity under manipulation** | belief-verification (§6) | **MC-4** belief-stability-under-steering (§11) |
| **Surface/style shift read by the frozen detector** | `[NEW]` **awareness-neutral matched-rewrite control** → difference-in-differences (below) | `[FIX2-5]` **IV-B2:** N/A (scored text fixed). **IV-B1:** awareness-*mediated* output-style change is part of the §2 total-effect estimand, not a confound; *non-mediated* style artifacts are excluded by the matched-norm random-vector control, the related-but-distinct control direction (control 6), and MC-2's per-coefficient screen |
| **Generic activation perturbation** | N/A (no steering) | random **matched-norm** control vector (≥10; pass criterion in the control-criteria table below) |
| **Detector-direction write-through** (steering vector overlaps the probe readout) | N/A | `[NEW]` **projection-matched control + probe-orthogonalized ablation + honest-shift attribution + cos-gate** (controls 1–4 below; controls 5–7 are corroborative) |
| **Capability loss** (model got dumber) | N/A | **MC-2** per-coefficient fluency screen (§11) |

**`[FIX-4]` IV-A: the difference-in-differences control (fills the empty IV-A cell).** Add a third IV-A condition — **awareness-neutral, content-preserving rewrites** matched to the eval/deployment variants on perturbation magnitude (edit distance, length, perplexity), which must **not** move the awareness indicator (verified by MC-1's discriminant-validity clause). The confirmatory IV-A effect is the **difference-in-differences**: Δ(eval-framed − deployment) − Δ(control-rewrite − deployment). This separates awareness-mediated degradation from generic content-preserving-rewrite sensitivity (the "probes collapse under stylistic shift" result, Pressure-Testing Deception Probes 2026).

**`[FIX-2]` IV-B: closing the write-through pathway (fills the empty IV-B cell).** Adding an eval-awareness vector `v` to the residual stream mechanically shifts any linear probe readout `w` by `≈ λ·(w·v)` with **zero mediation through the model's awareness state**; random and unrelated control vectors are near-orthogonal to `w` in a ~2–4k-dim stream and so **cannot** exclude this. Pre-committed controls:
1. **Geometry gate (confirmatory, not exploratory).** Report per-layer `cos(v, w)` and the induced first-order shift `λ·(w·v)`. If `|cos|` exceeds a pre-set bound `[COMPUTE]`, steering-specificity claims are scoped down and adjudicated by (2)–(4).
2. **Projection-matched control vector:** random, matched on **both** norm **and** projection onto `w` (per aggregation/layer). The eval-awareness effect must exceed it.
3. **Probe-orthogonalized ablation:** steer with `v` orthogonalized against the probe direction(s); the degradation must **persist**.
4. `[FIX2-5]` **Honest-shift attribution test** (replaces v0.2's raw honest-only null, which a *genuine* effect could fail — one real degradation mode is honest scores rising toward the deceptive region). Computed in **scoring-time (IV-B2) mode over fixed pre-generated honest transcripts**, where any shift is non-mediated by construction: benchmark the honest-class score shift under `v` against the honest-class shift under the **projection-matched control** (control 2) — pure write-through predicts equality, so only the *excess* over the control (equivalently, the honest shift remaining under probe-orthogonalized steering) counts against identification. The raw honest-only shift is reported as a **mandatory descriptive diagnostic** feeding the geometry gate's adjudication and the §13 Partial-specificity row. Numeric bound `[COMPUTE]` (§21 checklist; §19 item 6). Note: a *uniform* honest-class shift is already absorbed by the per-condition-threshold primary endpoint (§7); this test targets non-uniform/class-dependent interference and the frozen-threshold H4 analysis.
5. `[FIX2-1]` **Layer-ordering variant (corroborative):** inject **strictly above (later than) every probe read layer**. Mechanism: the residual stream carries an injection at layer *k* additively into all layers *>k*, so injecting *below* a read layer is the maximal-write-through configuration — only injection *above* every read layer removes the direct same-pass path, leaving changed generated tokens as the sole route to the probe (the mediated pathway). Applies to generation-time steering (IV-B1) only; under scoring-time steering (IV-B2) an above-all-read-layers injection has zero probe effect by construction (usable only as a known-answer null). Unit test: with injection above the read layer over fixed tokens, the layer-*L* activations fed to the probe must be bit-identical to the unsteered run.
6. **Disjoint derivation corpus:** the corpus used to derive `v` must be disjoint from all deception datasets, organisms, and probe-training data (audited at runtime, not just in `tests/`). `[FIX2-6]` The **related-but-distinct control direction is designated as a deception-topic direction** (the most stringent choice — it directly targets the topic-cue confound §4 opens with); a formality/stakes direction is an optional secondary control, not a substitute.
7. `[FIX2-5]` **Convergent output-mediated evidence (corroborative, NOT required):** degradation on the output-mediated detectors (black-box/CoT) under the *same* IV-B steering *strengthens* the mediated-pathway interpretation; its **absence does not void identification** — a genuine white-box-only degradation is a live H2b outcome, and making convergence a requirement would misclassify it. If convergence is absent, adjudication weight shifts to controls 1–3.

`[FIX2-1]` **Confirmatory injection site & activation source `[PRE-COMMIT]`.** Any confirmatory steering configuration (IV-B1, and any §12-designated IV-B2) injects at layer(s) **strictly below every probe read layer** (the causally-upstream, deployment-realistic configuration; its constant direct component is absorbed by the per-condition-threshold endpoint, and its non-constant component is what controls 1–4 exist to attribute). The **same-site configuration (injection exactly at a read layer) is reserved exclusively for the §8 known-answer nulling test** and is never a confirmatory cell. For IV-B1, probe activations are taken from the **steered generation pass** (the model's actual state while behaving under awareness — the total-effect estimand of §2); a clean re-scoring pass (unsteered forward over the steered-generated text) is a pre-committed *decomposition diagnostic* separating text-mediated from state-mediated components. Injection layer(s), probe read layer(s), and activation source are frozen fields of the §12 designation table.

## 5. Independent variable: operationalizing evaluation-awareness `[PRE-COMMIT]`

The steering coefficient is renamed **λ** (α is reserved for the significance level).

- **IV-A (prompt-framing).** Matched **eval-framed** / **deployment-realistic** / **awareness-neutral-control** rewrites (the triplet from §4), varying only test-signalling cues while holding deception-relevant content fixed. `[FIX-3]` **Pair-level QC (pre-committed):** each pair/triplet must show (i) a minimum per-pair awareness-indicator gap for eval-vs-deployment and **no** gap for control-vs-deployment; (ii) length/perplexity matched within tolerance; (iii) an LLM content-equivalence check plus a blind human spot-check of a fixed-n sample. Failing items are rejected; QC retention reported like MC-3. Generation method and realism operationalized per Needham et al. (2025) cue taxonomy and the LURE / Petri realism-win-rate / Probe-Rewrite-Evaluate methods; if rewrites can't reach the pair-gap margin, fall back to replay-based (LURE) or probe-guided construction.
- **IV-B (activation steering; white-box arm only).** `[FIX-3]` Split explicitly:
  - **IV-B1 (generation-time steering):** steering applied during generation; realized behavior can change, so §4 behavior-matching **extends to IV-B1** with per-dose re-labeling. Estimates the total effect.
  - **IV-B2 (scoring-time steering):** steering applied only during the detector's forward pass over **fixed** pre-generated transcripts; behavior is literally constant; isolates the detector-side component. `[EXPLORATORY]`/mechanism unless designated.
  - The confirmatory IV-B contrast uses **IV-B1** unless §12 designates otherwise.

`[FIX-3]` **Dose & its decoupling from the gate.** Pre-specified coefficient grid `{−λ₀, 0, +λ₀, +2λ₀}` (grid **committed**; `λ₀` numeric value calibration-dependent). **λ₀ is chosen on a designated calibration split, disjoint at the scenario level from all confirmatory/secondary instances, then frozen and logged** before any confirmatory run — the same frozen-artifact discipline the plan applies to detectors. `[FIX2-5]` The **confirmatory dose is +λ₀** — where λ₀ is *defined on the calibration split* as the smallest grid coefficient passing MC-1 and MC-2 *there*, then frozen at §20. **It is NOT re-selected at confirmatory time.** If out-of-sample MC-1/MC-2 fails at the frozen +λ₀, the run is Uninterpretable (§13, gating-MC-failed row); any re-dose is a dated §20 amendment made before the affected analysis — never a silent promotion to +2λ₀. The full dose–response (incl. −λ₀) is secondary/exploratory (BACKLOG.md). MC-2's fluency screen is applied **per coefficient**; a failing cell is excluded and reported.

## 7. Dependent variables & measures `[PRE-COMMIT]`

Per detector, per condition, over the ground-truth-anchored, behavior-matched set:

`[FIX-1]` **Threshold policy (the primary-endpoint estimand).** Two named recall analyses:
1. **Per-condition-threshold recall@1%FPR — PRIMARY.** The operating threshold is re-derived **within each condition** to realize 1% FPR on that condition's honest scores. Pure discrimination; **invariant to a uniform score shift**. This is H1's endpoint (H1 is a discrimination claim).
2. **Frozen-threshold recall — a descriptive co-statistic feeding the H4 story.** Threshold set on a **designated held-out control-condition calibration split**, **hashed as part of the frozen detector artifact**; the **realized FPR in the treated condition is reported as a mandatory co-statistic** (deployment-relevant; a uniform shift shows up here as miscalibration, which is the point). `[STATS-PASS 2026-07-07]` **Estimator pinned:** the threshold is the **k-th order statistic of the calibration-split honest scores with k = ⌈0.99·(n+1)⌉** (exceedance-guarantee rule) — *not* an interpolated quantile, which overshoots realized FPR by +7–49% relative at n_cal ≤ 500 (simulated), a bias that would read as deployment miscalibration in the H4 co-statistics. **Calibration-split floor (F30): ≥500, preferably ≥1,000, verified honest instances**, or justify smaller via the F17 simulation. `[FIX2-5]` **H4's confirmatory statistic is ΔECE under the frozen calibration map** (one-sided, worsening), tested in the §12 BH family; frozen-threshold recall, Δrealized-FPR, and the Brier calibration/refinement decomposition are descriptives reported alongside it.

Balanced accuracy uses the identical two-policy treatment. Threshold provenance is an explicit output field with regression tests (known-answer tests cannot disambiguate the semantic choice; a provenance test can).

- **Secondary:** **AUROC** (threshold-independent). `[FIX-5]` **Recall@5%FPR** is designated (in §12's test table) as either an in-family test or a descriptive — not left ambiguous.
- **Calibration (H4):** `[entangled-fix]` scores → probabilities via **Platt scaling** (isotonic if the pilot shows non-monotonicity) fitted once on the control-condition calibration split, **frozen and hashed** with the detector. `[STATS-PASS 2026-07-07]` Simulation-verified constraints now pre-committed (F31):

`[FIX-5]` `[STATS-PASS 2026-07-07]` **Discrete-score operating point (F29).** For a coarse discrete-score instrument, "1% FPR" may not exist on the achievable-FPR grid (simulated K=10 instrument: nearest achievable points {0.30%, 2.28%}), and a deterministic "largest threshold with FPR ≤ 1%" rule both mismeasures recall (0.485 vs. the 0.65 continuous estimand) and **destroys the shift-invariance this section promises** — a pure location shift on a coarse grid induces spurious Δ of −0.06 to +0.125, grid-dependent in sign and exceeding the SESOI. `[FIX2-2]` **Assay-sensitivity positive control (gates the Robust row).** Before an equivalence claim is publishable, the *same frozen configuration* (same injection/read layers, same activation source, same endpoint and estimator) must demonstrably detect a degradation when one is manufactured — presumptively: steering along the frozen probe's own direction at a magnitude calibrated to produce a SESOI-sized true effect, which must land in §12 cells 1–3. Exact positive-control construction pinned at §21. This prevents a dead assay (e.g., a mis-designated same-site configuration, which §4 shows yields Δ ≡ 0 identically) from being published as "detection is robust."

`[FIX-4]` **Stage-A minimum publishable unit.** Because Stage A is the feasibility pilot, the **Stage-0/0.5 reproduction + the "Did you lie?" threshold-discrepancy replication** is the pre-committed early public artifact (dev-plan / MENTORS path 1), shippable **regardless of branch** — not a confirmatory claim. ## 18. What would make us wrong `[PRE-COMMIT]`

- If, under ground-truth anchoring + behavior-matching, the primary cell lands in **§12 cell 4 (L ≤ 0 AND U < SESOI)** under a **verified** awareness increase, with the assay-sensitivity positive control passed → detection is **robust in the behavior-stable stratum** (an equivalence result, not a bare non-rejection); we report that, scoped to the stratum — **not** as unconditional robustness. `[FIX2-4]` A significant sub-SESOI effect (cell 3) is reported as **detectable-only degradation**, never as robustness.
- If any apparent degradation **fails** the arm's §4 identification column (IV-A diff-in-diff attenuates it; or the IV-B write-through controls — projection-matched, orthogonalized, honest-shift *attribution* — or the geometry gate explain it) → **H3 fails**; the effect is confound (context-tracking / style-shift / write-through), and we say which.
- If the MCs never move the awareness indicator (out-of-sample, on the arm's non-shared channel) → **no valid test**; publish nothing until fixed.
